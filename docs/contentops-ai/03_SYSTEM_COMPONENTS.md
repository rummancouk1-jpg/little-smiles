\# Core Content Entities



\## TopicCluster

Strategic long-term content category.



Examples:

\- newborn care

\- sleep

\- feeding



Purpose:

Establish topical authority.



\---



\## TopicBrief

Pre-generation strategic content brief.



Contains:

\- search intent

\- target keyword

\- audience pain points

\- tone guidance

\- internal linking targets



Purpose:

Acts as the contract between SEO strategy and AI generation.



\---



\## BlogPost

Published content artifact.



Purpose:

Primary public-facing content object.



\---



\## BlogRefresh

Historical content improvement record.



Purpose:

Track meaningful content updates over time.
